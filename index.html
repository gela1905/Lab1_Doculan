Welcome to Cavite State University

Don Severino delas Alas Campus

Nestled in the heart of Indang, Cavite, Cavite State University stands as a beacon of knowledge, innovation, and excellence in the province. Since its humble beginnings, CvSU has grown into one of the region's leading institutions of higher learning, shaping generations of thinkers, leaders, and changemakers.

Guided by its mission to provide quality and relevant education, CvSU continues to nurture students in fields ranging from agriculture and engineering to information technology and the sciences — instilling not just knowledge, but also integrity, service, and pride in the Cavitenio spirit.
